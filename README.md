## Software Description

**AI Job Coach** is a web application designed to help college students prepare for internships and full-time jobs. Users can upload their resume and cover letter alongside a job description, and the app uses AI to analyze the documents and deliver targeted, actionable feedback based on the specific job requirements.

The feedback helps students improve alignment between their documents and the job posting, refine their wording and tone, and boost the overall quality of their application materials. In addition to document review, AI Job Coach helps students practice realistic interview scenarios, building confidence before they step into the real thing.

User accounts, resumes, and cover letters are securely stored using a Supabase database. The application is built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on the frontend, with Node.js and Express powering the backend logic.
